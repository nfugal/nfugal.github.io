**NateFugal.tech** is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

### 1. Conformance Status
The [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility. This site aims to be conformant with **WCAG 2.1 level AA**.

### 2. Technical Specifications
Accessibility on this site relies on the following technologies:
* HTML / CSS
* JavaScript (Minified for performance)
* The "Minimal Mistakes" Jekyll Theme

### 3. Known Limitations
Despite our best efforts, some content may not yet be fully adapted. This may include:
* **Legacy Blog Posts:** Older posts (pre-2023) may lack comprehensive Alt-Text on images. We are auditing these manually.
* **Third-Party Embeds:** Widgets or scripts from external sources may not fully meet WCAG standards.
