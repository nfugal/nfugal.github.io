*Last Updated: May 2026*

This privacy policy explains how **NateFugal.tech** collects and uses data. Because this site is a static site hosted on GitHub, we aim to collect as little data as possible.

### 1. Information We Collect
* **Hosting Logs:** This site is hosted by **GitHub Pages**. GitHub may collect system logs from visitors, including IP addresses, to maintain the security and integrity of the service.
* **Comments (Staticman):** If you leave a comment, we collect the name and message you provide. If you provide an email for notifications, it is processed via **Staticman** and stored in our GitHub repository.
* **Analytics:** We use [Google Analytics/None - *Choose one*] to understand site traffic. This may use cookies to track return visits.

### 2. How We Use Your Information
We use your information only to:
* Display your comments on the site.
* Ensure the technical security of the website.

### 3. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)
Depending on your location, you have the right to:
* Request a copy of the data we have stored (e.g., your comments).
* Request that we delete your data/comments.
* Object to processing.

To exercise these rights, please contact me at info [at] natefugal [dot] tech.
